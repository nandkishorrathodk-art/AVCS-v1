## 🌟 Executive Concept & Creative Direction

### The Core Premise
Current computer-use AI agents interact with computers like a tourist looking through a telescope: they take a massive 2-megabyte screenshot every few seconds, upload it to a distant cloud server, wait 4 seconds, and guess coordinates. It is slow, expensive, and fragile.

**AVCS (Artificial Visual Cortex System)** approaches the problem through the lens of **biological visual neuroscience**. Human eyes do not send full uncompressed screenshots to the brain; they process a continuous, 15-millisecond ocular stream of temporal motion, dynamic changes, and foveal gaze fixation right on local hardware.

### The Scientific Boundary (Zero Hype, 100% Truth)
* **What AVCS is:** An experimental Python research framework exploring local screen-based visual perception, temporal visual modeling (`TemporalVisualCortex`), and goal-conditioned gaze grounding (`GoalConditionedGrounder`).
* **What AVCS is NOT:** It is not a single monolith model, and it is **not yet a proven, general-purpose computer-use model** for arbitrary desktop tasks.
* **System 1 vs System 2:** System 1 (AVCS) is the fast ocular perception layer. Motor actions are a separate experimental layer. System 2 is high-level deliberate reasoning (an LLM).
* **The Evidence:** 570+ passing tests prove software specification and mathematical contract adherence—not open-world benchmark mastery.
* **The Mission:** 100% Open Source under Apache-2.0 to invite global researchers to inspect, test, and build together.
